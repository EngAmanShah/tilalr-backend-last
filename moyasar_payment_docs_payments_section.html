Create Payment

POST 
/payments

Start a new Card, Apple Pay, Samsung Pay or STC Pay payment. The source object will define what payment is started.

When a payment is started, the status will be one of:

  • initiated
  • paid
  • failed

If the payment is initiated then you need to complete the payment transaction by completing the payment challenge found in source.transaction_url depending on the payment source type.

Request​

  • application/json

Body

required

    given_id string

    A UUID (v4 is recommended) that you generate from your side and attach it with the payment creation request to support idempotency.

    It is going be the ID of the created payment.

    amount integerrequired

    A positive integer representing the payment amount in the smallest currency unit.

    Examples:

    • 1.00 SAR = 100
    • 1.00 KWD = 1000
    • 1 JPY = 1
    currency
